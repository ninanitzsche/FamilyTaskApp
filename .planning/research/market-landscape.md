# Market Landscape — Family Kanban Boards

## Overview
The family organization app space is crowded but fragmented. Most apps focus on one aspect.

## Key Competitors (2026)
| App | Kanban? | Chores | Calendar | Groceries | Meal Planning | Gamification | Pricing |
|-----|---------|--------|----------|-----------|---------------|--------------|---------|
| **Famiboard** | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | Stars | Free / $9/mo |
| **NestQuest** | ✅ Task Board | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | XP, Streaks | Paid |
| **Cathold** | ✅ 5 columns | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | Avatars | $7.99/mo |
| **NestBoard** | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | Badges | $4.99/mo |
| **Kinstead** | ✅ Quest Board | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ | Gold/Rubies | $12/mo |

## Key Insights
1. **Kanban for families is underserved** — Only 3 apps offer kanban in a family context
2. **Gamification drives engagement** — XP, streaks, rewards
3. **All-in-one wins** — Calendar + Chores + Groceries + Meals is standard
4. **PWA is dominant architecture** — Families use phones, tablets, kitchen kiosks

## Differentiation Opportunity
- **Flight Levels-inspired design** — FL1 (individual), FL2 (family coordination), FL3 (goals)
- **ADHD-friendly design** — No competitor addresses neurodivergent family needs
- **Session-first** — Built around cleaning sessions, not task lists
